# Hi, I'm Amanda

I spent 15+ years in video post-production, including more than a decade on globally broadcast scripted television, running the systems, workflows, and partner relationships that kept creative teams delivering on deadline. I'm now bringing that operations work into technology, partner, and creative organizations.

## What I work on

- **Operations and systems**: triage, intake, and routing for requests from many teams; shared storage, permissions, and access (Avid NEXIS)
- **Partner and vendor operations**: onboarding outside partners to standards, delivering materials with role-based access, and holding work to timelines
- **Enablement**: SOPs, playbooks, and training that teams adopt and reuse; 30+ colleagues trained and onboarded
- **Creative operations**: resourcing, scheduling, feedback cycles, and version control for high-volume creative work
- **Process improvement**: root-cause fixes, trackers, and documentation that keep problems from coming back
- **AI-native workflows**: investigation, summaries, documentation, and generative video, always with human review
